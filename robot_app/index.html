    About RP1
    RP1 humanoid · 29 rigid links, 28 torque-controlled joints, 41.1 kg, ≈1.4 m tall. Mesh MJCF lives in RP1_description/mjcf.
    Legs 6 DoF each: hip pitch / roll / yaw → knee → ankle pitch / roll. Torso 2: waist roll + yaw.
    Arms 7 DoF each: shoulder pitch / roll / yaw → elbow → wrist roll / yaw / pitch.
    Actuators are limited from 32 N·m (wrists) to 140 N·m (hips / knees / waist); friction + damping modelled per joint (damping 0.01–0.03, armature 0.003–0.029).
    An IMU site sits in the pelvis; feet are approximated by 7 capsule contacts each (see the Colliders toggle); the whole robot pins its soles to the ground via the Ground pin, and the yellow ball tracks the centre of mass — watch it drop when you squat.
